## moogbass

# patch: moogbass

THE iconic Moog ladder-filter sound. saw + 25% cutoff + 20% res, no envelope on filter, no LFO. static patch, fat low end.

## knob positions

fill in after the first session — snap a panel photo and either describe or attach.

- VCO 1: SAW, 16' (or 32')
- VCO 2: OFF
- mixer: VCO1 full
- filter cutoff: ~25%
- filter resonance: ~20%
- envelope amount on filter: 0
- VCA envelope: short attack, full sustain, medium release
- LFO: OFF
- spring reverb: OFF for raw pass, ~30-40% wet for spring pass

## vibe descriptor

one-liner you'd recognize this patch by, in three years.

## external CV / patching

none for v1. fully internal patch.

## BPM / sync

free / no sync.

## moogbass-smooth

# patch: moogbass-smooth

The "polite" cousin of moogbass. Same patch architecture, eased velocity + slower attack. Same fat low end, less transient bark.

## differences from moogbass

- velocity: 75 (vs 100) — softer note-on, less VCA punch
- VCA envelope attack: slightly slower than moogbass — rounds off the initial transient
- everything else identical: VCO 1 saw 16'/32', filter cutoff ~25%, res ~20%, env amount on filter 0, LFO off

## use this version when

producer wants the Moog character without the snap — sustained bass parts, melodic basslines, parts where the bark would compete with kick or vocal transients.
