# patch: moogbass

THE iconic Moog ladder-filter sound. saw + 25% cutoff + 20% res, no envelope on filter, no LFO. static patch, fat low end.

## knob positions

fill in after the first session — snap a panel photo and either describe or attach.

- VCO 1: SAW, 16' (or 32')
- VCO 2: OFF
- mixer: VCO1 full
- filter cutoff: ~25%
- filter resonance: ~20%
- envelope amount on filter: 0
- VCA envelope: short attack, full sustain, medium release
- LFO: OFF
- spring reverb: OFF for raw pass, ~30-40% wet for spring pass
