# patch: moogbass-smooth

The "polite" cousin of moogbass. Same patch architecture, eased velocity + slower attack. Same fat low end, less transient bark.

## differences from moogbass

- velocity: 75 (vs 100) — softer note-on, less VCA punch
- VCA envelope attack: slightly slower than moogbass — rounds off the initial transient
- everything else identical: VCO 1 saw 16'/32', filter cutoff ~25%, res ~20%, env amount on filter 0, LFO off

## use this version when

producer wants the Moog character without the snap — sustained bass parts, melodic basslines, parts where the bark would compete with kick or vocal transients.
